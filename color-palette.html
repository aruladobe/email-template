How to read this palette

Dark text (#1A1A1A) is used on steps 0–30 for Blue & Gray, steps 0–20 for Red, steps 0–30 for Yellow, and steps 0–20 for Green — where luminance is high enough to ensure readable contrast.

White text (#FFFFFF) starts at step 40 for Blue & Gray & Yellow, step 30 for Red, and step 30 for Green.

Outlook Desktop (2013–2021) ignores @media (prefers-color-scheme) and [data-ogsc]. Its OS-level inversion algorithm can flip mid-scale colours unpredictably. Always test against an Outlook renderer before sending.
